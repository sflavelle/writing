Waking up from being effectively dead/in a coma by being shaken awake was not a pleasant experience, you decided.

When your glassy eyes vacantly stared at him, barely processing that you were awake and alive, Jevil cursed himself. He quickly got to work, carefully lifting you onto his back and carrying you from the room in which you had apparently been resting.

Slowly, as you were hurriedly carried out of some civilian building out onto the streets, you started to become aware of what was happening around you.

People running about in panic.

The sounds of demolition and explosions in the distance.

And blue and red strings *everywhere* you looked.

Also, hang on... weren't you *taller* than your travel companion? Not... shorter, apparently?

You mumbled into his shoulder, "s'mthin happening...?"

"Oh, oh!" Jevil immediately responded with growing delight, turning his head to nuzzle into the top of yours. "Welcome back, my dear friend! It's good to have you with me once more! Sadly this reality is under attack, attack! So we must away, away!"

You took a few moments to process his words. "By... runnin'...?"

"Just for a moment! Using my magic *here* will surely attract the wrong attention, so we must find a safe place to do so. Please hold tight!" Once the imp felt your legs and arms tighten around him, he grinned and increased his pace, running down the street.

You were both coming up on a park, and Jevil immediately ducked into it, hiding amongst the trees and bushes as they followed a path into its depths.

The sounds of the chaos happening in the city faded to rumbles as he brought you deeper into the park, and your senses started to sharpen, the fog of a long sleep lifting.

He finally stopped, carefully setting you down before checking you over. “You are unharmed,” he stated, matter-of-factly. “Good, good.” He turned, summoning his Devilsknife. “When I bring us to our new realm, hold tight, tight!”

It was as if that was the signal.

As he began to swing the blade down to create his new portal, a flash of white rushed past you—

“Hrk…!”

The Devilsknife dropped to the dirt. Pain erupted from your chest.

A skeleton had appeared, with an unzipped jacket and a target marker over his heart and he—
He had plunged a red knife right into Jevil’s back.

“…Uee hee hee…” the jester chuckled darkly. “A visitor, visitor…! Do you wish to play a little numbers game? If your—“

Another, larger skeleton erupted from the edge of the clearing, battle axe at the ready and charging for Jevil. You screamed his name but—

A dull thud sounded out as the axe was embedded in Jevil’s body.

“your number… is zero.” The second skeleton chuckled. “i win.”

Jevil’s body seemed to glitch for a moment, and you felt something tear in your chest as you stared at the carnage—

Before he disappeared, and two halves of a joker card fluttered to the ground in his place.

You stared at the remains of the torn card, clutching your chest, then at his killers as the bigger one slowly turned toward you. The first was staring at the card in confusion.

“The fuck was *that*?!” The smaller said to his companion, gesturing at the card. “The least he coulda done is dusted like any other monster!”

The rest of his rant faded into the background as the hulking monster slowly stepped closer, a singular blood red… pupil? Shining from his skull.

Horror settled in to your body.

And you promptly blacked out.
